Classic Beef Wellington

  1. Home
  2. Recipes
  3. Beef Wellington
45 min 4 Servings Advanced

About This Recipe

The Beef Wellington is the crown jewel of British cuisine — a tender beef tenderloin wrapped in a rich mushroom duxelles and encased in a golden, flaky puff pastry. This recipe from our head chef delivers restaurant-quality results at home.

Instructions

  1. Season the beef fillet generously with salt and pepper. Sear in a hot pan with oil for 2 minutes on each side until browned.
  2. Prepare the mushroom duxelles by finely chopping mushrooms and cooking with shallots and thyme until all moisture has evaporated.
  3. Lay out plastic wrap, spread a thin layer of Parma ham, then spread the mushroom mixture evenly.
  4. Place the seared beef at one end and roll tightly into a log shape. Refrigerate for 30 minutes.
  5. Wrap the beef log in puff pastry, sealing the edges with egg wash. Score the top lightly.
  6. Bake at 200°C (400°F) for 25–30 minutes until pastry is golden. Rest for 10 minutes before slicing.
Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 800g beef fillet
  • 400g chestnut mushrooms
  • 6 slices Parma ham
  • 500g puff pastry
  • 2 shallots, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • Fresh thyme
  • 2 eggs (for egg wash)
  • Salt and black pepper
