About This Event

Join us for an unforgettable evening of culinary excellence and fine wines. Our head chef has crafted a special 5-course tasting menu designed to perfectly complement our curated selection of international wines. Each course is paired with a wine chosen by our sommelier to enhance the flavors and create a harmonious dining experience.

This intimate event is limited to 40 guests to ensure a personalized and memorable experience. Dress code is smart casual.
